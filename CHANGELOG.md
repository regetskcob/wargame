# Changelog

What each store version of Panzergefecht brought, newest first, as its most
important points. The build number follows in brackets; the web version at
<https://panzergefecht.de/play/> is always the latest `main`. The
store texts for a release live in `store/ios/metadata/*/release_notes.txt`.
The German version is [`CHANGELOG.de.md`](CHANGELOG.de.md); keep both in
step, point for point.

## Unreleased

- The browser tab of the game and the landing page shows the app icon
  again instead of Horizont's glyph
- The mini map follows the round again: tanks, guns, aircraft and crates
  move on it instead of the picture standing still after the first frame
- Other players' tanks stop when nothing more comes from them instead of
  driving on straight across the field, as they did on the end screen
  after a lost battle

## 1.8.0 (17) – 2026-10-26

- Panzergefecht has its own address: the game, room links and QR codes
  now point to <https://panzergefecht.de/play/>, and links shared before
  keep opening the app
- A new look on the design system Horizont, in the game and on the
  landing page: calm olive surfaces with hairlines, signal yellow only for
  the one main action and the selection, new type and every number in
  Monaspace
- New app icon on every platform, with launch screens, the Apple TV top
  shelf, the Android TV banner and the iMessage icons to match: the tank
  from the side on the horizon
- Every symbol in menus, the HUD, the crates, the tutorial, the television
  and the watch comes from Horizont's thin, sharp set
- The waiting room as in the design: the room code big, crews with their
  count, the vehicles as cards with their values as bars; on the iPad it
  uses the whole screen upright and sideways
- On iPhone, iPad and Mac deleting the account, the controls and the
  pairing code open as the system's own dialogs
- Squads on foot cross the river of the defense map on the bridges
  instead of walking through the water
- The host's NEW GAME takes along everybody still on the end screen,
  where guests were left behind in the waiting room before
- On the television the sign-in card on the start page is one focus
  stop, not two, and imprint and privacy notice name
  info@panzergefecht.de as the contact
- Rejoining a room after a dropped connection no longer closes the room
  channel again, and the Apple Watch and the Apple TV's focus frame are
  steadier

## 1.7.0 (15) – 2026-10-18

- The closed zone of a battle holds for 30 seconds and then draws in to
  nothing, with damage rising every second, so every round comes to an end
- A battle started alone in the room fills up with CPU tanks instead of
  running on with nobody to fight
- Every wave beaten off beyond the eighth in a defense round earns 30
  experience, 15 when the base falls in the end
- The camera goes past the edge of the defense field, so the panel no
  longer covers the tank and the crates at the top
- The German texts say "Gewonnen" instead of "Sieg" everywhere, from the
  round's end to the leaderboard, the watch and the Live Activity
- Room links from the web open the Mac app, and a Mac never counts as a
  phone, however small its window

## 1.6.1 (13) – 2026-10-12

- On the Apple TV the Menu button opens a round menu: in a defense round
  the panel with every gun and upgrade, and always the way out, walked with
  swipes and pressed with a click or play/pause
- The aim assist and the comrades take on a helicopter when nothing else is
  in range, so the last helicopter of a wave no longer holds it open for good
- The tutorial's controls cards can be tried out with the remote and
  controllers on a television, and a click presses the button in focus
- Text fields on a television open the keyboard only when clicked, and the
  account page says when the sign-in mail is on its way
- CPU tanks squeezing past a building by an island's shore are no longer
  pushed into deep water

## 1.6.0 (12) – 2026-10-11

- Two new grounds: the mountain forest with dense woods, scree and hills,
  and the islands, more water than land, joined by wooden bridges with
  shallows that tanks wade through slowly
- Two new defense maps, Flussufer and Haarnadel, and a ninth vehicle from
  rank 6: the Igel, a rocket launcher with four rockets a salvo, also among
  the enemies from wave 6
- CPU tanks drive what the people in the round drive, so every rank meets
  its match; in red against blue players on AUTO are spread by vehicle, so
  a new player plays alongside a veteran
- Whenever CPU tanks come, at least as many as there are people; they even
  out sides left short, in the same vehicles on both sides, and comrades in
  a defense round drive the vehicles of their wave
- Nobody can play in somebody else's name any more: every message carries a
  tag only its sender can make, and who hosts a room comes from the server
- When the host of a defense round leaves for good, the next player takes
  over waves, base and guns; rounds against CPU tanks count for the time
  the server measured
- Mortars and howitzers lead their shells onto where a target will be, so
  a mortar stops tanks on the road
- A replay can show the whole map from above (*Whole map* or the key M)
- Guests see the host's difficulty, terrain and teams in the waiting room,
  and things have one name everywhere: coins, guns and upgrades
- Fixes: a waiting room no longer closes after a pause in a background
  tab, the start page holds still, sounds no longer come late in a busy
  defense round, and a second thumb takes over a stick

## 1.5.0 (11) – 2026-10-11

- Trenches are manned: three riflemen and a machine gun fire at soldiers
  and tanks, each aiming for himself and looking out where the enemy comes
  from, and from wave 4 one with an anti-tank rocket launcher; on the
  road a trench lies across it from edge to edge, tanks of either side
  crawl over it and stay slow a moment after, and enemy shells now wear
  it down
- Squads on foot patrol instead of standing on their spot and stalk enemy
  tanks and soldiers that come within sight
- Ranks take longer: every rank starts from 0 and needs the step the card
  used to show as the total, and the card counts within the rank
- The last enemy helicopter of a wave comes in to the defenders instead of
  hanging back far out, and tank shells hurt it a little more
- The defense shop marks nothing after use: buttons are only bright when
  affordable or faint; building says what a gun or trench is for, and a
  notice says when the funds start to reach
- Guns and soldiers lead their aim on soldiers walking across their fire
- The replay's end button sits in the bar beside the next tank
- Deleting the account comes last on the account page

## 1.4.1 (10) – 2026-10-11

- Tablets use their screen in the menus: the start page and the waiting
  room start at the top and take the whole width, two columns where there
  is room, vehicle cards that grow with the bars beside them
- Large tablets such as the 13" iPad draw the menus larger, as the HUD
- The account is a page of its own with two columns instead of a sheet
  that scrolled, and one way to close it
- A change of language reaches every text at once, the legal links and the
  pages below the account included
- Tablets no longer show a vibration switch they have no motor for

## 1.4.0 (9) – 2026-10-10

- A calmer, slimmer look: frames only on the outer panels, frameless boxes
  for modes, sections and tank cards, hairline buttons, outlined small icons
- Account sheet: the call sign is saved with the tick or Enter, registering
  takes that name, the language sits in the title line
- Guests find signing in on a card on the start page
- Holding out longer in a lost battle earns experience and shows the place
- The phone as a controller carries the whole defense shop
- Only the app on a phone becomes a controller, never a tablet or a browser
- Two players on one screen: each half wears its team colour
- CPU tanks see no further than people at night, in fog and in sand
- Sounds fade with distance, long defense rounds run smoother, rooms are
  guarded against floods, forged messages and short dropouts
- Apple Watch: every mode alone against CPU tanks, counted for the account
  on the iPhone

### Fixes

- Account: the e-mail field offers the saved address, the keyboard no longer
  stays up after closing the sheet, the language chip follows a tap, the
  call sign's label is no longer cut off
- Rooms: one host when a browser opens its room in two tabs, guests see the
  host's mode in the waiting room, players arriving by a room link count
  against the Realtime budget, an iPad with two paired phones no longer
  floods the server
- Split screen: starts only once the second player is in the round (no
  empty half), frames in team colours, the same mode and end screen for
  both, named second player, never offered on phones
- HUD: the aim assist stays on when the HUD is rebuilt, a touch brings the
  sticks back over a controller, enemy arrows no longer twitch or slide over
  gauges, map and panels, the inventory clears the defense panel on keyboard
  screens, notices clear the sticks
- CPU tanks drive the vehicles of their level and attack after the gentle
  start, so doing nothing no longer wins
- Stats: accuracy stays at most 100 %, a rating change of zero is hidden,
  the leaderboard hides empty and guest rows and no longer jumps
- The start button stays in place when a replay appears, the difficulty
  picked last is kept on the device
- Remote repairs, rapid fire and crate pickups are checked against what is
  possible
- The engine sound is no longer released twice

### Penetration and stress test ([#12](https://github.com/regetskcob/wargame/issues/12))

- Rooms cap messages per sender and kind and check what comes in: forged
  round starts, room closings, blasts, grenades, endless speeds and floods
  of made up tanks, aircraft, drones, guns, troops or items no longer get
  through
- A player who drops out for up to six seconds keeps tank, guns and, as
  host, the defense; deaths from before a rematch no longer count
- A builder now learns when the host destroyed their gun
- Call signs lose invisible and right to left characters
- Migration 0018: Realtime slots capped under a budget kept in the
  database, rated opponents only while online, kills and damage tied to
  the time since the last round, at most 300 rounds a day, badges stamped
  with the server's time
- A player arriving by room link is counted against the Realtime budget
- Long defense rounds no longer pile up fallen soldiers, craters, stains,
  wrecks and replay positions
- Faster drawing: on an iPad Pro 2018 a defense at wave 30 went from about
  65 to 95 fps
- A second phone paired during the results no longer leaves an empty half,
  and the inventory stays clear of the defense build panel
