Panzergefecht has its own address: panzergefecht.de. A new look throughout, with calm olive surfaces, new type and thin, sharp symbols, and a new app icon on every device. The waiting room shows the room code big and the vehicles as cards, and on the iPad it uses the whole screen. Infantry crosses the river on the bridges, and rejoining a room after a dropped connection is steadier. In multiplayer, tanks no longer drive on by themselves at the end of a round, and the result comes right after the last kill.
