BETA DESCRIPTION
Panzergefecht is a top-down tank duel for iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple TV and Apple Watch: single player against CPU tanks, multiplayer in a shared room, defense against eight waves and more, and capture the flag, red against blue.

WHAT TO TEST (1.8.2)
• Tanks drive about a tenth slower: does it feel better?
• Menus: the chosen option of a choice row is clearly marked; on the Apple TV the menus are larger and keep clear of the screen edge
• No Live Activity appears after a round any more, the result in the iMessage chat still arrives
• Multiplayer fix: after a lost round the other tanks no longer drive on straight, and won/lost comes at once, also after a single heavy hit
• The mini map moves with the round
• New design: calm olive surfaces, new type, slim symbols and the new app icon. Does it all fit together, is any text cut off or a button hard to hit?
• Waiting room: the room code big, crews with their count, vehicles as cards with their values as bars; on the iPad upright and sideways
• Own address: room links and QR codes point to panzergefecht.de. A QR code scanned with the iPhone camera opens the app, not Safari
• Multiplayer: after a round the host's NEW GAME starts the next round for everyone still on the end screen
• Dialogs: deleting the account, the controls and the pairing code open as the system's own dialogs
• Defense: infantry crosses the river on the bridges instead of walking through it
• Apple Watch (Series 9 or later, watchOS 26): the new look, play a round with the crown
• Apple TV and Mac: the start page and a round with the remote, a controller or the keyboard
