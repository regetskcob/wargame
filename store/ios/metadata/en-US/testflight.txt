BETA DESCRIPTION
Panzergefecht is a top-down tank duel for iPhone and iPad: single player against CPU tanks, multiplayer in a shared room and defense against eight waves and more.

WHAT TO TEST
• Controls: drive with the left thumb, aim and fire with the right, aim assist on and off
• Portrait and landscape during play and in the menus
• Defense: build turrets, upgrade them, buy upgrades, extend after wave 8
• Day and night alternate during a battle
• Account: play as a guest, quit and restart the app, secure the account with e-mail (enter the code from the mail)
• Sound: follows the silent switch and the volume keys
• Apple Watch (Series 9 or later, watchOS 26): install it from the Watch app on the iPhone, steer with the crown, play a round
