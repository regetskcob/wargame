The first version for Android: Panzergefecht with four ways to play. Alone against CPU tanks on easy, normal or hard, against each other with up to four pilots, as a squad against waves with guns and a base, or red against blue in capture the flag. Nine vehicles, six grounds with weather and time of day. Invite friends by code, QR code or link, and turn the phone into a controller. Touch with aim assist, controller or keyboard. With an account: rank, experience and the leaderboard.
